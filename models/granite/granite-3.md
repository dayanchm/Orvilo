# Granite 3

**Released:** October 2024 onward

## Overview

Granite 3 is IBM's compact open model generation for enterprise language, code, retrieval, and safety workloads.

## Architecture and Training

The family includes dense 2B and 8B language models, sparse MoE variants, and later 1B/3B editions, generally with long-context support. Specialist models cover code, vision, time series, and safety.

## Best Fit

Use Instruct checkpoints for assistants and RAG, Guardian for risk classification, and specialist checkpoints only for their documented modality.

## Limitations

Model sizes and capabilities differ across the broad Granite catalog. Enterprise use still requires evaluation, governance, and data-access controls.
