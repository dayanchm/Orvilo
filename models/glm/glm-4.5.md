# GLM-4.5

**Released:** July 2025

## Overview

GLM-4.5 is an open-weight mixture-of-experts family designed for reasoning, coding, and agents.

## Architecture and Training

The flagship has about 355B total and 32B active parameters; Air is smaller. Hybrid reasoning lets applications select deliberate or direct responses, and the family supports 128K context.

## Best Fit

Choose the flagship for maximum quality and Air for lower serving cost. Both target tool-driven software and research workflows.

## Limitations

MoE serving remains infrastructure-intensive. Validate tools, code, and long-context behavior on the exact checkpoint.
