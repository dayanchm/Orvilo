# GLM-4

**Released:** January 2024 onward

## Overview

GLM-4 is a broad generation spanning hosted frontier models and open GLM-4-9B checkpoints.

## Architecture and Training

Open variants include chat and long-context editions, while related vision models add image and video understanding. The ecosystem supports tools, code, multilingual tasks, and up to 128K context on relevant variants.

## Best Fit

Use the 9B checkpoints for local work and Z.ai-hosted variants when higher capability or managed tools are needed.

## Limitations

The GLM-4 label covers several checkpoints; modality, context, license, and availability are not interchangeable.
