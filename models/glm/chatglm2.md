# ChatGLM2-6B

**Released:** June 2023

## Overview

ChatGLM2 improved the compact assistant's performance, context length, and inference efficiency.

## Architecture and Training

It retained about 6B parameters, expanded standard context to 32K, used multi-query attention, and updated training and alignment.

## Best Fit

It fits bilingual chat, summarization, and local experimentation where compatibility with the ChatGLM ecosystem matters.

## Limitations

Long-context availability depends on the checkpoint and runtime. Generated Chinese and English facts still require verification.
