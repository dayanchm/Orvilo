# GLM-130B

**Released:** August 2022

## Overview

GLM-130B is an open bilingual English-Chinese foundation model and a large-scale demonstration of the General Language Model architecture.

## Architecture and Training

The 130B dense model uses autoregressive blank infilling and was trained on English and Chinese corpora. THUDM released weights and code for research.

## Best Fit

It is primarily useful for historical study, bilingual scaling research, and reproduction of the GLM approach.

## Limitations

Its short context and very large hardware footprint make later GLM releases more practical.
