# GLM-5

**Released:** February 2026

## Overview

GLM-5 scales Z.ai's open agentic model line for complex reasoning, coding, and software-engineering workflows.

## Architecture and Training

The released sparse model has roughly 744B total and 40B active parameters, with a 200K context. Its post-training emphasizes agents, tool use, and long-horizon task execution.

## Best Fit

It is best suited to large-scale inference systems and agent research that can support expert-parallel deployment.

## Limitations

The model is costly to store and serve, and autonomous actions need permission boundaries, monitoring, and rollback plans.
