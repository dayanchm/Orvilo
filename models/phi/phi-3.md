# Phi-3

**Released:** April 2024

## Overview

Phi-3 turned Phi into a broader production-oriented family of small open models.

## Architecture and Training

Phi-3 Mini has 3.8B parameters, while Small and Medium expanded to roughly 7B and 14B. Mini checkpoints were released with 4K and 128K context; a Phi-3 Vision variant added image understanding.

## Best Fit

The family targets on-device assistants, retrieval, summarization, coding, and applications where latency or memory matters.

## Limitations

Capabilities and context differ by checkpoint. Small models still need grounding, safety controls, and domain evaluation.
