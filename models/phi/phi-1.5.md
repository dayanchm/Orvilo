# Phi-1.5

**Released:** September 2023

## Overview

Phi-1.5 extended the Phi data-quality approach from code toward common-sense reasoning and general language tasks.

## Architecture and Training

The 1.3B model used synthetic textbook-style data and selected web data. Its results helped show that small models can learn reasoning patterns without training on every benchmark format.

## Best Fit

It is useful for research, lightweight generation, and studying synthetic-data training. Later Phi releases are better production defaults.

## Limitations

Small size does not eliminate hallucination, bias, or weak world knowledge; evaluate it within its 2K context.
