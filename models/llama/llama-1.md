# LLaMA 1

**LLaMA 1**, released by Meta in February 2023, was a research-focused family of dense, decoder-only Transformers at 7B, 13B, 33B, and 65B parameters.

The 33B and 65B models were trained on 1.4T public-data tokens. Architectural choices included RMSNorm, SwiGLU, rotary position embeddings, and causal self-attention. The project argued that carefully trained smaller models could compete with much larger systems.

LLaMA 1 was not an instruction-following assistant and used a non-commercial research license. Community fine-tuning nevertheless made it highly influential in open-model research.
