# Llama 3.2

**Llama 3.2**, announced in September 2024, split into two groups:

- 1B and 3B text models for edge and mobile deployment
- 11B and 90B vision-instruct models for image and text understanding

The vision models attach a trained image encoder to the Llama language backbone and support tasks such as visual question answering, document understanding, and image captioning. The lightweight text models make local summarization, rewriting, and tool workflows possible on constrained devices.

This was Llama's first official multimodal generation.
