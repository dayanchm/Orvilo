# Llama 2

**Llama 2**, released in July 2023, expanded the family to 7B, 13B, and 70B parameters and introduced official **Llama 2-Chat** checkpoints.

The models used a 4K context and were pretrained on 2T tokens. The 70B version adopted grouped-query attention. Chat models were adapted with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning from human feedback.

Llama 2 broadened commercial availability under the Llama 2 Community License. It made self-hosted assistants practical, while still requiring application-level safety controls and factual verification.
