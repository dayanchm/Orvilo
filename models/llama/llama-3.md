# Llama 3

**Llama 3**, released in April 2024, began with 8B and 70B dense text models in pretrained and instruction-tuned forms.

It introduced a 128K-token vocabulary, grouped-query attention at both sizes, and training on more than 15T tokens. The initial checkpoints used an 8K context. Improved data curation and post-training strengthened reasoning, coding, and instruction following.

Llama 3 remained text-only. Later Llama 3.x releases added much longer context, more sizes, multilingual support, and vision.
