# Mistral Large

**Mistral Large**, introduced in February 2024, was Mistral AI's original flagship commercial language model. It focused on multilingual reasoning, code, mathematics, instruction following, and function calling.

Unlike Mistral 7B and Mixtral, the original Large model was API-accessible rather than an open-weight release. Its 32K context and strong European-language support positioned it for enterprise assistants and retrieval applications.

“Large” is a product family name; later Large 2 and Large 3 are distinct generations.
