# Mixtral 8x22B

**Mixtral 8x22B**, released in April 2024, scales the sparse MoE design to eight 22B experts, with two selected per token.

The model has roughly 141B total and 39B active parameters, a 64K context, multilingual ability, and strong code and mathematics performance. Base and instruction checkpoints use the Apache 2.0 license.

Its weights remain large even though inference activates only part of the network.
