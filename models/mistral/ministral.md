# Ministral

**Ministral** is Mistral's compact-model family for edge, local, and high-throughput deployment.

The 2024 generation introduced 3B and 8B text models with 128K context. The Mistral 3 generation later added Apache-2.0 Ministral 3B, 8B, and 14B checkpoints with text-and-image capability.

These models trade frontier capability for lower memory, latency, and deployment cost. They suit extraction, local assistants, function calling, and privacy-sensitive workflows.
