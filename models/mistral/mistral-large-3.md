# Mistral Large 3

**Mistral Large 3**, released in December 2025, is an Apache-2.0 multimodal MoE model with **675B total parameters and 41B active per token**.

It accepts text and images and was released in base and instruction-tuned forms. The return to sparse experts follows the original Mixtral line while scaling capacity to a frontier open-weight model.

Large 3 targets general conversation, multilingual work, vision, coding, and enterprise customization.
