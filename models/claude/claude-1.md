# Claude 1

**Released:** March 2023

## Overview

Claude 1 was Anthropic's first broadly announced assistant model, designed for dialogue, writing, analysis, and coding with an emphasis on helpful, honest, and harmless behavior.

## Architecture and Capabilities

Its alignment approach grew from Constitutional AI: written principles and AI-generated feedback supplement human feedback. The generation included later updates such as Claude 1.3 and expanded long-context support.

## Best Fit

This model established Claude's conversational style and safety-oriented training. It is historically important but has been retired from the Claude API.

## Limitations and Lifecycle

Use a current model for production. Early Claude models have weaker reasoning, tool use, vision, and reliability than later generations.
