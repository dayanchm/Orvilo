# Claude Opus 4

**Released:** May 2025

## Overview

Claude Opus 4 was the high-capability Claude 4 model for difficult coding and agentic tasks.

## Architecture and Capabilities

It used hybrid reasoning, vision, tool use, and a 200K context. Anthropic emphasized sustained work on complex software-engineering tasks.

## Best Fit

It targeted advanced agents, codebases, research, and multi-step professional work.

## Limitations and Lifecycle

The original model is retired. Autonomous tool use still requires least privilege, monitoring, and approval gates.
