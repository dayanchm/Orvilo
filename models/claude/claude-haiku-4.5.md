# Claude Haiku 4.5

**Released:** October 2025

## Overview

Claude Haiku 4.5 brought near-frontier coding and computer-use capability to Anthropic's fast tier.

## Architecture and Capabilities

It supports hybrid reasoning, vision, tool use, and a 200K context while prioritizing low latency and cost.

## Best Fit

It fits responsive assistants, customer service, pair programming, and worker agents operating under a stronger orchestrator.

## Limitations and Lifecycle

Speed can encourage more actions per minute; maintain the same permission and validation controls used with larger models.
