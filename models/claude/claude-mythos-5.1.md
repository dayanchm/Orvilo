# Claude Mythos 5.1

**Released:** September 2026

## Overview

Claude Mythos 5.1 is Anthropic's restricted frontier model for vetted cybersecurity and biology researchers.

## Architecture and Capabilities

Anthropic reports gains in cybersecurity and biology over Mythos 5. It is deployed through trusted-access and verification programs rather than general public access.

## Best Fit

The model is designed for advanced defensive and scientific work that requires carefully governed access to dual-use capability.

## Limitations and Lifecycle

Only approved organizations can use it. Strong operational security, logging, containment, and human oversight remain essential.
