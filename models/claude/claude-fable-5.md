# Claude Fable 5

**Released:** June 2026

## Overview

Claude Fable 5 provides Mythos-class underlying capability for general users with defense-in-depth safeguards.

## Architecture and Capabilities

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 share an underlying model. Fable adds classifiers and fallback behavior for sensitive cybersecurity, biology, chemistry, and model-distillation requests.

## Best Fit

It targets ambitious coding, knowledge work, research, and prototyping that benefit from capability above the Opus tier.

## Limitations and Lifecycle

Some benign requests may trigger safeguards or fall back to another model. Availability briefly changed after launch and was restored globally in July 2026.
