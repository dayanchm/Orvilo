# Claude 3 Haiku

**Released:** March 2024

## Overview

Claude 3 Haiku was the fastest and least expensive member of the Claude 3 family.

## Architecture and Capabilities

It supports text and image input, text output, and a 200K context window. It targeted responsive classification, extraction, customer support, and high-volume processing.

## Best Fit

Haiku introduced useful multimodal intelligence at a low-latency tier.

## Limitations and Lifecycle

It is retired from the Claude API. Vision quality, reasoning depth, and agentic reliability trail newer Haiku models.
