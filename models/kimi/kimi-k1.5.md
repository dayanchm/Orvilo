# Kimi K1.5

**Released:** January 2025

## Overview

Kimi K1.5 is a multimodal reasoning model trained to improve both short and long chains of thought.

## Architecture and Training

Moonshot's report emphasizes reinforcement learning, long-context scaling, and joint reasoning over text and images. It can solve visual mathematics, document, and general reasoning tasks.

## Best Fit

Use it to study multimodal reinforcement learning and long-context reasoning; hosted Kimi products may expose additional tools.

## Limitations

Weights and full training artifacts were not released in the same way as Kimi K2. Benchmark results do not guarantee reliability on new tasks.
