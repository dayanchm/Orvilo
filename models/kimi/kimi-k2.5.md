# Kimi K2.5

**Released:** January 2026

## Overview

Kimi K2.5 extends K2 into a native multimodal agentic model that can reason over text, images, and video frames.

## Architecture and Training

It retains the 1T-total/32B-active sparse design and continues pretraining on roughly 15 trillion mixed visual and text tokens. Agent-oriented post-training supports coding, tool use, and coordinating complex tasks.

## Best Fit

Use it for visual agents, interface understanding, multimodal research, and long coding workflows where its infrastructure needs are acceptable.

## Limitations

Open weights do not make outputs automatically safe. Review the modified license terms, tool permissions, and visual-data privacy.
