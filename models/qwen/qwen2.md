# Qwen2

**Qwen2**, released in June 2024, included 0.5B, 1.5B, 7B, 57B-A14B MoE, and 72B models.

It expanded training beyond Chinese and English to 27 additional languages and improved coding and mathematics. The 7B and 72B instruction models supported up to 128K context.

Qwen2 combined dense and sparse architectures in one family and strengthened the base for later specialist code, math, audio, and vision models.
