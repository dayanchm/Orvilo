# Qwen1.5

**Qwen1.5**, released in February 2024, improved alignment, multilingual ability, and developer compatibility.

It offered dense models from 0.5B through 110B and a Qwen1.5-MoE model. The main family uniformly supported up to 32K context and integrated directly with Hugging Face Transformers without custom remote code.

Broad size coverage and official quantized formats made Qwen1.5 useful from local devices to large servers.
