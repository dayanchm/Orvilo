# Qwen3

**Qwen3**, released in April 2025, introduced hybrid thinking and non-thinking modes in one family.

Dense sizes range from 0.6B to 32B. MoE variants include **30B-A3B** and **235B-A22B**, where the suffix records active parameters per token.

The models support more than 100 languages, coding, mathematics, tool use, and agents. Initial releases used 32K context; 2507 updates support 256K and can be extended to 1M.

Post-training combined long chain-of-thought data, reasoning RL, thinking-mode fusion, and general RL. Users can trade response speed against reasoning depth without switching model families.
