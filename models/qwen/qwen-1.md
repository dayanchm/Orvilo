# Qwen 1

**Qwen 1**, released by Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team in 2023, established an open-weight bilingual Chinese-English model family.

Models were released at 1.8B, 7B, 14B, and 72B parameters in Base and Chat forms. Depending on size, context reached up to 32K. Training used up to 3T multilingual tokens, while chat models added instruction alignment, tool use, and code-interpreter behavior.

Qwen 1 established the family's focus on multilingual language, code, mathematics, agents, and practical quantized deployment.
