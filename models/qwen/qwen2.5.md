# Qwen2.5

**Qwen2.5**, released in September 2024, expanded the dense family to 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B, 14B, 32B, and 72B models.

It improved coding, mathematics, structured data, JSON generation, multilingual instruction following, and long-context handling up to 128K on supported checkpoints. Base and Instruct variants allow either further training or assistant use.

The generation also supported specialist families such as Qwen2.5-Coder and Qwen2.5-Math.
