# Falcon 180B

**Released:** September 2023

## Overview

Falcon 180B scaled the Falcon design to 180B parameters and was one of the largest openly released pretrained models of its period.

## Architecture and Training

TII trained it on roughly 3.5 trillion tokens using the RefinedWeb-centered data pipeline. Base and chat-oriented variants were published with a 2K context.

## Best Fit

It is mainly valuable for large-scale inference, fine-tuning research, and studying 2023 open-model scaling.

## Limitations

Deployment needs multiple high-memory accelerators. Review the Falcon license and do not confuse downloadable weights with unrestricted open-source software.
