# Gemma

**Released:** February 2024

## Overview

Google's first Gemma generation translated technology developed for Gemini into compact, openly released model weights.

## Architecture and Training

The decoder-only family launched at 2B and 7B parameters in pretrained and instruction-tuned forms. An 8K-token context and small footprints made experimentation possible on laptops, workstations, and cloud accelerators.

## Best Fit

Use Gemma for text generation, summarization, question answering, and fine-tuning research. It is a text-only model: image understanding arrived in later generations.

## Limitations

Gemma is open-weight under Google's Gemma terms, not an OSI open-source software license. Generated facts still require verification.
