# OLMo 2

**Released:** November 2024 onward

## Overview

OLMo 2 improved training stability, data quality, and downstream performance while preserving the project's end-to-end openness.

## Architecture and Training

Ai2 released 7B and 13B models, later extending the generation to 32B. Training uses updated Dolma data and openly documented annealing and post-training stages.

## Best Fit

It is well suited to language-model research, transparent fine-tuning, and auditing how data and optimization affect behavior.

## Limitations

Base, Instruct, and preference-trained checkpoints serve different purposes. Its standard context is shorter than many frontier APIs.
