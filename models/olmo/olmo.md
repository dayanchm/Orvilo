# OLMo

**Released:** February 2024

## Overview

OLMo made the complete language-model development pipeline available for scientific study, not only the final weights.

## Architecture and Training

Ai2 released 1B and 7B models with the Dolma corpus, training code, checkpoints, logs, and evaluation tools. The architecture is a conventional decoder-only Transformer chosen for reproducibility.

## Best Fit

Use OLMo to study pretraining, data provenance, checkpoints, and reproducible evaluation.

## Limitations

The first generation has shorter context and weaker instruction behavior than later commercial or open models.
