# OLMo 3

**Released:** November 2025

## Overview

OLMo 3 extends Ai2's open stack into long-context, instruction, and reasoning research.

## Architecture and Training

The generation includes 7B and 32B variants and releases training data, recipes, checkpoints, and post-training resources. Its architecture mixes local and full attention layers, while specialized Think checkpoints expose reasoning-oriented behavior.

## Best Fit

Use it when traceability and reproducibility matter: researchers can connect behavior to data and training stages more directly than with weights-only releases.

## Limitations

Even a transparent model can hallucinate or reproduce data biases. Context and behavior vary among Base, Instruct, and Think checkpoints.
