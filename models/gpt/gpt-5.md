# GPT-5

**GPT-5**, released in 2025, unified strong general intelligence with configurable reasoning for coding and agentic tasks.

The family included GPT-5, GPT-5 Pro, GPT-5 Mini, and GPT-5 Nano. Applications could trade capability, latency, and cost while retaining a common generation of instruction following and tool use.

GPT-5 supports reasoning effort controls, function calling, structured outputs, image input, and multi-step tool workflows. Pro spends more computation on difficult problems; Mini and Nano target cost-sensitive or high-throughput work.

Its key shift was making reasoning and agent behavior part of the mainstream GPT family rather than a separate experimental line.
