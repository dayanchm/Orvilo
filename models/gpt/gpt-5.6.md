# GPT-5.6

**GPT-5.6** is a tiered model family for professional and agentic work.

| Variant | Goal |
|---|---|
| GPT-5.6 Sol | Flagship capability |
| GPT-5.6 Terra | Balance intelligence and cost |
| GPT-5.6 Luna | Cost-sensitive workloads |

The family supports configurable reasoning, text and image input, a **1.05M-token context window**, up to 128K output tokens, function calling, structured outputs, and advanced tool workflows.

The tier names express deployment tradeoffs, not published parameter sizes. Sol is best for complex work; Terra for balanced applications; Luna for faster, cheaper volume.
