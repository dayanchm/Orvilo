# GPT-4o Mini

**GPT-4o Mini**, released in 2024, is the smaller, faster, and more affordable member of the GPT-4o family. It targets focused tasks such as classification, extraction, summarization, lightweight coding, and high-volume chat.

It supports text and image input, text output, structured outputs, function calling, and a 128K context window. Its low cost made it useful for replacing older small models and for systems that route simple work away from a flagship model.

Smaller models trade capability for efficiency. Complex reasoning, ambiguous instructions, and difficult coding may require a stronger model or additional verification.
