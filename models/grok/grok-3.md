# Grok 3

**Released:** February 2025

## Overview

Grok 3 introduced a stronger pretrained model plus Grok 3 mini and explicit reasoning modes.

## Architecture and Training

xAI reports training on the Colossus supercomputer with much more compute than its predecessor. Reinforcement learning supports extended reasoning, while DeepSearch combines reasoning with web and X retrieval.

## Best Fit

Choose the main model for broad capability, mini for cost-efficient reasoning, and DeepSearch when answers require current sources.

## Limitations

Reasoning traces can still contain errors, and search summaries must be checked against their cited sources.
