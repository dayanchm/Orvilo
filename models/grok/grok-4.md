# Grok 4

**Released:** July 2025 onward

## Overview

Grok 4 is xAI's frontier reasoning generation, with model and product variants aimed at difficult analysis, coding, search, and agents.

## Architecture and Training

The family includes high-capacity reasoning offerings and later Grok 4 Fast variants. Grok 4 Fast supports reasoning and non-reasoning operation, integrated web/X search, and a context window of up to two million tokens.

## Best Fit

Use reasoning variants for difficult multi-step tasks and Fast when throughput, long context, or tool-driven search matters.

## Limitations

Specifications are variant-specific and mostly API-hosted. Confirm the current model card, context, pricing, and tool availability.
