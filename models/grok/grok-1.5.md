# Grok 1.5

**Released:** March 2024

## Overview

Grok 1.5 substantially improved mathematical reasoning, coding, and long-context processing over the first Grok release.

## Architecture and Training

xAI announced a 128K-token context and evaluated retrieval across long sequences. Parameter count and full training details were not publicly disclosed.

## Best Fit

It is an important transition from the initial model toward production-grade reasoning and document workflows.

## Limitations

Grok 1.5 was a hosted model rather than an open-weight release; availability and product behavior can change.
